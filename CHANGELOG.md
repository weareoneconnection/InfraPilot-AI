# Changelog

## 2.1.0 — 2026-10-19
- Seven-workspace field intelligence operating interface for command, reporting, risk events, spatial twin, independent re-inspection, analytics and governed-agent collaboration.
- Re-inspection photo upload restored across the full evidence-comparison card.
- Failed re-inspections now expose actionable reasons and block resubmission until a new photo is selected or generated.
- Competition report, evidence boundary, four-minute script, source snapshot and UTF-8-safe delivery package synchronized to the production baseline.
- 34 deterministic benchmark cases and 41 automated tests.

## 2.0.0 — 2026-10-17
- Asset registry with geofences; location by asset id, QR payload, GPS or photo EXIF.
- Bilingual word-boundary risk rules; fusion with reporter and validated vision; never lowered; stop-work flag.
- Response and closure SLAs by risk; overdue detection.
- Evidence-routed specialists over governed knowledge packs; cited findings or explicit gaps.
- Named-human approval, dispatch with owned tasks, task completion by named people.
- Independent re-inspection verifier with 7 checks (new photo by SHA-256 and perceptual hash, after dispatch, within geofence or attested, independent inspector, tasks done, hazard cleared).
- Precedents from closed events; dashboard (SLA compliance, first-pass verification, hotspots).
- MCP server for agents and inspection robots (reporting and reading only).
- Synthetic photo generator; 34-case benchmark; 39 tests at the original 2.0.0 baseline; mobile-first console.
